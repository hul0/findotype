# Support & Sponsorship

Thank you for using and exploring **Findotype**!

---

## 1. Getting Help & Reporting Issues

* **Bug Reports & Feature Requests**: Please open an issue on the [GitHub Issue Tracker](https://github.com/hul0/findotype/issues). Include your Python version, OS, and minimal reproducible example.
* **General Discussions & Questions**: Open a discussion thread on [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/hul0/findotype/discussions).

---

## 2. Supporting the Project

Findotype is an independent, open-source medical ontology engine and research project built to provide offline biomedical and clinical intelligence tools without paywalls or telemetry.

If you or your organization are interested in **supporting, sponsoring, or partnering** on this project:

* **Email**: **[hulo@crine.in](mailto:hulo@crine.in)**

We welcome support for:
- Sponsoring server infrastructure and CI compute.
- Integrating additional clinical ontologies (MONDO, Orphanet, DrugCentral, ChEMBL).
- Domain-specific medical informatics research and validation.
- Academic collaborations and grants.

---

## 3. Security Vulnerabilities

If you discover a security vulnerability or critical issue, please do not disclose it publicly on the issue tracker. Email details directly to **[hulo@crine.in](mailto:hulo@crine.in)**.
